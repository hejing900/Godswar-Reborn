# Wonderland treasure reward log

A newly committed treasure-box claim writes one left-side log line per reward, for example `Obtained Level 4 Sapphire x 2.` Item names come from the process-pinned item catalog and quantities come from the durable claim receipt. The authoritative bag snapshot is sent first. AlreadyClaimed receipts still refresh the current bag but do not announce another award. Existing claim authority, durable reward contents, useful failure responses, and the initial claim menu are unchanged. Success sends neither ServerNote nor the native function58 result dialogue.

The log uses the installed client's text-only 137-byte opcode10038 frame: formatter50, channel1, two terminated 64-byte strings at offsets9 and73. `SrvMsg.lua` defines `CHANNEL_PRESONAL = 1` at line5, concatenates `name .. note` for formatter50 at lines975-976, and calls `GameAPI:AddPersonalMessage_UTF8(strText,6,1)` for that channel at lines1142-1143. This does not invoke any item acquisition or loot acknowledgment handler. It avoids the native optimistic bag changes associated with opcode10185 and local-player opcode10050.

The receiver and Lua dispatch were already audited under `artifacts/wonderland-red-announcement-20260911`; the current installed-script excerpts, hash, and independently calculated personal-log frame are recorded under `artifacts/wonderland-treasure-log-20260911`. These are native-script-backed, server-authored messages, not a claim of newly captured external reward-log packets.
